INVESTING • RESEARCH NOTE

What happens when value investing meets momentum investing?

Published January 2026

Value and momentum are often portrayed as opposing forces in the investing world. But what if they're not mutually exclusive?

This research explores how a disciplined investor might combine value principles (buying quality at discount) with momentum awareness (understanding price trends) to build a more robust investment approach.

Key findings:

  • Historical data shows value + momentum combinations can outperform either strategy alone over certain periods
  • The key is timing: recognizing when value traps emerge and when momentum is breaking down
  • Position sizing and diversification matter more than being "pure" to either philosophy

VALUATION • RESEARCH NOTE

DCF: the model is only as good as the assumptions

Published December 2025

Discounted Cash Flow analysis is one of the most powerful valuation tools available. It's also one of the most misused.

The problem isn't the model—it's the assumptions. Small changes in terminal growth rate, margins, or WACC can swing a valuation by 30-50%.

This note walks through a real company example showing how assumption sensitivity works and why scenario analysis (base case, bull case, bear case) matters far more than precision in a single DCF model.

PORTFOLIO • RESEARCH NOTE

Building a portfolio around risk, not predictions

Published November 2025

Most investors build portfolios around return expectations. Better investors build around risk management.

Why? Because predicting returns is nearly impossible. Managing risk is not.

This framework explores how to think about position sizing, correlation, and diversification in a way that protects capital first and lets returns follow naturally.

EQUITY RESEARCH

How Fiscal Frontier approaches equity research

Our equity research process starts with business quality: understanding competitive advantages, management quality, and financial health.

We then apply valuation discipline, asking not just "what is this worth?" but "what price offers adequate margin of safety?"

Finally, we stress-test the thesis. What would break this investment? What has to go right? This keeps us honest about assumptions and risks.

PORTFOLIO STRATEGY

Portfolio strategy is more than picking stocks

Individual stock selection is only one part of portfolio management. Equally important are asset allocation, diversification, and rebalancing.

A well-constructed portfolio can deliver superior risk-adjusted returns with less volatility than a portfolio of individually "good" picks.

The key is thinking systemically: how do all the pieces work together?

QUANT • BACKTESTING

Testing an investment idea before trusting it

Backtesting isn't about finding the perfect strategy. It's about filtering out the bad ones before you risk real capital.

We test momentum, trend-following, mean reversion, and multi-factor strategies against 20+ years of market data to understand their behavior in different environments: bull markets, bear markets, high inflation, low inflation.

The goal is to build conviction in an approach before committing real money.
